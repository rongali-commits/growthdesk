# GrowthDesk Portfolio Case Study

## Title

GrowthDesk: a complete client-service operating system

## Short description

Designed and built a production-ready white-label platform that connects customer enquiries, lead management, follow-up automation, client delivery, reviews, and reporting.

## The problem

Service businesses often manage one customer journey across a website form, personal inboxes, spreadsheets, reminder tools, file links, invoice messages, and review requests. Information gets copied by hand, next actions become unclear, and customers experience the business as a series of disconnected messages.

## The solution

GrowthDesk brings the full journey into one product:

- A polished customer-facing website and enquiry assistant
- Structured lead capture and a visible sales pipeline
- A unified team inbox with suggested next actions
- Connected follow-up, booking, delivery, and review automations
- Separate private client portals for progress, decisions, files, and invoice visibility
- Customer feedback and testimonial management
- Revenue and operations reporting
- Central white-label business settings

## My role

I handled the complete zero-to-one product process: problem definition, information architecture, interface design, application engineering, persistent data modeling, product copy, testing, documentation, marketplace packaging, and production deployment.

## Technology

Next.js, TypeScript, React, Tailwind CSS, Cloudflare D1-compatible SQL storage, Wrangler, Docker, GitHub, and Railway.

## Production result

The final product is deployed as a working production application with persistent data, responsive customer and staff interfaces, live API workflows, a public health endpoint, and a repeatable white-label configuration model.
