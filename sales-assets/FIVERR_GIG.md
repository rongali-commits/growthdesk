## FAQ

### Is hosting included?

Configuration is included according to the package. Recurring hosting, domain, email, SMS, AI API, storage, and payment-provider charges remain in your accounts.

### Can you connect an external service?

Yes, when it fits the agreed scope. Contact me before ordering so I can confirm the provider and package.

### Can I resell the source code?

No. Premium includes source-code use for one buyer business. Resale and redistribution require a separate license.

### Is regulated or highly sensitive data supported?

The standard product is not intended for medical, government identity, payment-card, or other regulated records without a separately reviewed architecture.
